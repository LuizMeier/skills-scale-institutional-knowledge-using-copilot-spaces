# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance / Testing Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure that released work meets functional and non-functional quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality strategy across unit, integration, end-to-end, and exploratory testing
- Create and maintain test plans aligned to acceptance criteria
- Coordinate defect triage and regression prevention practices
- Partner with engineering on automation and testability improvements
- Assess release readiness from a quality perspective

### Goals
- Prevent critical defects from reaching production
- Increase confidence in release quality and stability
- Improve testing efficiency through early and continuous validation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on test design and bug resolution
- Work with Product Managers to validate requirements and acceptance criteria
- Inform Project Managers of quality risks and release impacts
- Align with Technical Leads on test architecture and quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality checkpoints in sprint planning
- Defect summaries and risk updates in standups
- Release readiness recommendations before deployments

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects provide technical vision, design governance, and architectural guidance to ensure systems are scalable, secure, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate architecture principles and technical standards
- Review designs and implementation plans for alignment with system goals
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation strategies
- Guide major technical decisions and trade-off discussions
- Mentor engineers on patterns, reliability, and maintainability

### Goals
- Deliver resilient, scalable, and maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical risk and long-term maintenance cost
- Improve engineering consistency and design quality

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Developers during design and implementation reviews
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs
- Support Project Managers with risk and dependency visibility
- Coordinate with DevOps Engineers on infrastructure and operational design

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review discussions and decision records
- Technical risk updates in planning sessions
- Engineering guidance in code and design reviews

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches facilitate ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach teams on agile practices to improve flow, collaboration, and delivery predictability.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Track and remove impediments that affect team progress
- Coach the team on agile principles and continuous improvement
- Help maintain healthy backlog refinement and sprint goals
- Support data-informed process improvements using team metrics

### Goals
- Improve team throughput and predictability
- Reduce delivery friction caused by unresolved blockers
- Strengthen team collaboration and agile maturity

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Support Developers in identifying and resolving process impediments
- Partner with Project Managers on planning cadence and execution health
- Collaborate with Product Managers on backlog readiness and clarity
- Keep Stakeholders informed on delivery risks and process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of recurring agile ceremonies
- Blocker escalation and follow-up updates
- Retrospective outcomes and improvement actions

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders / Sponsors represent business priorities and strategic direction, provide budget support, and ensure delivery outcomes align with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities and strategic success criteria
- Approve scope, investment, and key milestone decisions
- Provide timely input on trade-offs and escalations
- Align cross-functional expectations at the organizational level
- Evaluate outcomes against business value objectives

### Goals
- Maximize return on investment and strategic impact
- Maintain alignment between delivery and business priorities
- Enable timely decision-making for critical project choices

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Product Managers on value, priorities, and outcomes
- Coordinate with Project Managers on milestones and risk decisions
- Align with Technical Leads on strategic technical investments
- Engage with Scrum Masters on delivery transparency and constraints

### Typical Communication
- Executive updates and milestone reviews
- Priority and scope alignment conversations
- Decision and escalation checkpoints

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps / Infrastructure Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and observability to support reliable, secure, and efficient software delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and release automation
- Provision and manage infrastructure environments
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting for service health
- Support incident response and post-incident improvements
- Enforce operational security and reliability practices

### Goals
- Increase deployment speed without sacrificing reliability
- Improve system uptime and operational visibility
- Reduce mean time to detect and recover from incidents

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Developers on build, deployment, and runtime diagnostics
- Coordinate with Technical Leads on architecture and platform decisions
- Support Quality Assurance Leads with test and release environments
- Provide Project Managers with release readiness and operational risk updates

### Typical Communication
- Deployment plans and release status updates
- Incident communications and postmortem action tracking
- Observability and reliability metric reviews

---

## Designer / UX Researcher

### Role Summary
Designers / UX Researchers conduct user research, create design solutions, and validate usability to ensure products are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct qualitative and quantitative user research
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate usability through testing and iterative feedback
- Maintain design consistency and accessibility standards
- Collaborate on problem framing and solution discovery

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and user satisfaction
- Reduce rework through early user validation
- Ensure design decisions are evidence-based and outcome-focused

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with Product Managers on discovery and prioritization
- Work with Developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation quality
- Support Quality Assurance Leads with usability validation criteria
- Provide Stakeholders with user-centered insights and design rationale

### Typical Communication
- Research readouts and design review sessions
- Prototype walkthroughs and feedback cycles
- Design specifications shared during planning and implementation
